# Letter templates

Fill every bracket from the case file. Cut any line that doesn't apply. Keep each letter to one screen where possible: officials skim.

Tone across all rungs: polite, factual, specific. The firmness comes from dates, amounts, rule citations and a named next step, never from adjectives.

---

## Rung 1: First written complaint to the company

```
Subject: Complaint – [issue in 5–8 words] – [Account/Order/Policy/PNR no.]

To: [Customer care / Grievance cell], [Company name]

I am writing to formally register a complaint regarding [one-line description].

Details:
- Name: [Full name]
- [Account/Order/Policy/PNR] number: [xxxx]
- Registered mobile / email: [xxxx]
- Date of transaction / incident: [date]
- Amount involved: ₹[x]

What happened:
1. On [date], [fact].
2. On [date], [fact].
3. [Any earlier call/chat and its ticket number.]

Relief requested:
[Exact ask, e.g. "Refund of ₹14,200 debited twice on 3 October 2026 to my account ending 4417."]

Please acknowledge this complaint with a reference number and resolve it within [the applicable period, e.g. 30 days / 14 days / one month].

Attachments:
1. [Document]
2. [Document]

[Name]
[Date]
```

## Rung 2: Escalation to Nodal Officer / Grievance Officer / Appellate Authority

```
Subject: Escalation – Complaint ref. [no.] unresolved – [Account/Order/Policy no.]

To: [Principal Nodal Officer / Grievance Officer / Appellate Authority], [Company], [circle/region if relevant]

I raised complaint [ref no.] on [date] regarding [issue]. [It remains unresolved / I received a reply on [date] that does not address the issue because [one line]].

Timeline:
- [date]: [event]
- [date]: complaint [ref] filed
- [date]: [reply / no reply]

Under [rule, e.g. "the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, which require redressal within one month" / "IRDAI's 14-day resolution requirement" / "the RB-IOS, 2026 30-day reply period"], this complaint should have been resolved by [date].

Relief requested: [exact ask].

If this is not resolved by [date], I will escalate to [RBI Ombudsman via cms.rbi.org.in / Insurance Ombudsman / the operator's Appellate Authority / the District Consumer Commission] without further notice.

<!-- Escalation date rule: for an ombudsman or appellate authority, use the date the user actually becomes eligible (e.g. 30 days after the bank RECEIVED the original complaint), never an earlier date. Threatening a forum the user can't yet reach is a bluff the company will recognise. For a parallel route like NCH, any reasonable date works. -->


Attachments: [previous complaint, reply, evidence]

[Name, contact, date]
```

## Rung 3: Ombudsman / regulator portal summary

Portals have character limits. Prepare two versions:

**Short (≈500 characters):**
```
Complaint [ref] filed with [Company] on [date] for [issue], ₹[x]. [No reply in 30 days / Rejected on [date] without valid reason]. Escalated to [nodal officer] on [date], ref [no.], unresolved. Seeking [relief] plus compensation for [time, expenses, harassment] as permitted under the scheme. Evidence attached.
```

**Full (for the description field or an attached letter):** reuse the Rung 2 letter, addressed to the Ombudsman, adding:
- A statement that the matter is not pending before any court, consumer commission or arbitrator.
- The compensation sought, split into the amount lost and compensation for harassment, time and expenses, within the scheme's caps.

## Legal-style notice before a consumer complaint

The user can send this themselves; a lawyer's letterhead adds weight for larger claims.

```
Subject: Notice under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 – [issue] – [ref no.]

To: [Company name], [registered office address], [grievance email]

Despite complaints dated [dates] (refs [nos.]), you have failed to [refund / repair / settle the claim / provide the service paid for]. This constitutes [deficiency in service / unfair trade practice] under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

I call upon you to [exact relief] within 15 days of receipt of this notice, failing which I will file a complaint before the [District] Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, [district], seeking [relief], compensation and litigation costs, at your risk as to costs.

[Name, address, date]
```

## Consumer Commission complaint (skeleton)

e-Jagriti generates parts of this; use the skeleton to prepare content.

```
BEFORE THE DISTRICT CONSUMER DISPUTES REDRESSAL COMMISSION, [DISTRICT]

Consumer Complaint No. ____ of [year]

[Name, age, address]                                  ... Complainant
versus
[Company name, registered office; branch address]     ... Opposite Party

COMPLAINT UNDER SECTION 35 OF THE CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2019

1. Parties: [who the complainant is; who the opposite party is].
2. Facts: [numbered, dated chronology].
3. Deficiency in service / unfair trade practice: [what the opposite party did or failed to do].
4. Cause of action: arose on [date] at [place], and continued on [dates of refusal].
5. Limitation: the complaint is filed within two years of the cause of action.
6. Jurisdiction: the consideration paid is ₹[x], within this Commission's pecuniary jurisdiction; the complainant [resides/works] in / the cause of action arose in [district].
7. Prayer: direct the opposite party to
   (a) [refund / replace / pay claim] of ₹[x] with interest at [rate]% from [date];
   (b) pay ₹[x] as compensation for mental agony and harassment;
   (c) pay ₹[x] towards costs of litigation;
   (d) any other relief the Commission deems fit.

List of documents: [numbered]

Verification and affidavit: [as generated by e-Jagriti, signed by the complainant]
```

## RTI application

```
To: The Public Information Officer, [Public authority, address]

Subject: Application under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005

Please provide the following information:
1. The current status of my [application/complaint] no. [x] dated [date].
2. Certified copies of file notings and correspondence relating to it.
3. The names and designations of officers who handled it, and the dates on which it moved between them.
4. [If rejected:] The reasons for rejection and the rule relied on.

Fee: Rs 10, paid with this application [by postal order no. ___ / online on submission]. I am / am not a BPL applicant.
(On RTI Online, leave this line out: the portal records payment, and its text box rejects the rupee sign.)

[Name, address, date]
```

## Response handling

- **Stalling reply** ("we are looking into it", "please allow more time" with no date): reply asking for a specific resolution date in writing; note the original deadline; escalate on schedule regardless.
- **Partial offer** (voucher, part refund, "goodwill gesture"): state what the rules entitle the user to, and ask whether they want to accept or hold out. Never accept on their behalf.
- **Rejection with reasons**: check the reasons against the rules and the user's evidence. Address each one in the escalation letter.
